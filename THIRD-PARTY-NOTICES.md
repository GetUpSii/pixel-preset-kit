## Not bundled (runtime prerequisites)

- **Aseprite** — proprietary/commercial pixel-art editor, required but not distributed here.
- **DeepSeek Harness (DSH)** and its `@deepseek-ai/*` plugins — installed by the user, not bundled.
- **Node.js** (>= 18) — runs the MCP server.
- **Python 3 with numpy + Pillow** — used by the optional `vision/` and `tools/` scripts. DSH ships a
  usable interpreter at `%USERPROFILE%\.dsh\dsh-runtimes\dsh-primary-runtime\dependencies\python\python.exe`.
